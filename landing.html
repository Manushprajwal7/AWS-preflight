Scoring Rubric

Seven Weighted Dimensions of Appeal Success

Derived from publicly documented Seller Central failure patterns, legal case studies, and consultant playbooks.

22% WEIGHT

Root Cause Specificity

Flags vague excuses ("system error", "glitch"), blame-shifting ("buyer lied"), and denial of violation. Requires root cause operational ownership.

18% WEIGHT

Corrective Actions Completed

Flags future promises ("we will"). Verifies past-tense completed actions: unit quarantine, removal order IDs, buyer refunds, and supplier invoices.

20% WEIGHT

Preventive Systemic Controls

Checks for recurring audit cadences (weekly/monthly), named responsible manager roles, updated SOP documentation, and supplier verification policies.

12% WEIGHT

Evidence & Invoices

Verifies presence of authentic distributor names, invoice numbers, ASIN formats (B0XXXXXXXX), order IDs, and carrier tracking documentation.

10% WEIGHT

Tone & Objectivity

Flags emotional pleas ("ruining our family"), threats of arbitration or lawsuits, and flattery. Enforces objective, business-like prose.

10% WEIGHT

Structure & 3-Part Layout

Detects required headers both as line items and inline text. Evaluates chronological section flow and guards against wall-of-text formatting.

8% WEIGHT

Length & Density Sweet-Spot

Penalizes appeals under 150 words (fatally brief) or over 900 words (investigator fatigue). Targets the 250–700 word executive sweet spot.

The Critical Override Principle

Amazon appeal decisions hinge on binary disqualifiers. If an appeal contains even a single critical violation—such as denying the violation, blaming Amazon, or threatening legal action—Preflight immediately caps the final score at 49 (WOULD BE REJECTED), regardless of how well other sections are written.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to questions sellers and judges frequently ask.

Does Preflight guarantee that my account will be reinstated?

No tool can guarantee reinstatement because final adjudication belongs solely to Amazon Seller Performance. Preflight eliminates unforced structural and tone errors that cause automated first-pass rejections.

Does Preflight upload my confidential invoices or sales data?

Never. Preflight is built with a zero-trust architecture. All parsing, regex evaluation, scoring, and text highlights execute 100% client-side in your local browser JavaScript engine.

How is Preflight's rubric derived if Amazon's rules are secret?

While Amazon does not publish its internal manual, failure patterns are documented extensively across Amazon Seller Central forums and case studies. Preflight maps those verified patterns into deterministic rules.

Can I deploy this myself on AWS?

Yes! The frontend is packaged in preflight-amplify.zip for instant drag-and-drop hosting on AWS Amplify. Refer to our detailed Deployment Guide.
